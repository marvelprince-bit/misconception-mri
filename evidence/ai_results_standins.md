# AI rule-writer results with stand-in writers (no live model yet)

Generated by `python scripts/evaluate_ai.py --writer oracle|cheat|down --seeds 20` on the merged design (five topics).
One wrong rule is removed from the tool's list and a simulated student follows it: 24 hidden rules x 20 students = 480 sessions per row.

- **oracle** writes the true rule (what a good model should do).
- **cheat** memorises the answers it was shown and does correct maths everywhere else (what the hidden-answer check exists to catch).
- **down** behaves like an AI service that cannot be reached.

"final_wrong_pct" of 8.1% in every row comes from sessions where the engine named another known rule before the AI was ever called (the AI is only called when no known rule fits).
